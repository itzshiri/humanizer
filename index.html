Paste a paragraph or a whole essay, press Humanize, and the page strips out the writing habits that make text read like AI. Everything runs in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere.

The page also removes em dashes, Oxford commas, markdown symbols, chatbot openers like “Certainly!”, “not just X, but Y” patterns, and trailing “highlighting the importance of…” clauses.

This is a rule-based editor. It removes known patterns but can’t promise a text will pass any detector, so read the result and adjust it in your own voice before you hand it in.
